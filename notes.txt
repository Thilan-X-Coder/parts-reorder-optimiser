Gross demand is used, so a cancelled bulk order of 80,995 units on the final day remains in the raw series. It falls in the dropped partial final week. A production version would match C-invoices to their originals.


ADI in one line
ADI = weeks the product was alive ÷ weeks it actually sold
eg:-
Sold in 52 of 104 weeks → ADI = 2.0 → "a sale happens every 2 weeks on average."


CV² in one line

Look only at weeks with a sale.

CV  = standard deviation ÷ mean
CV² = CV × CV

Sales of 10, 11, 9, 10, 12 → tiny CV². Sales of 5, 300, 8, 250 → huge CV².
